Frequently Asked Questions

Do I need any gym equipment to use IntelliFit?

expand_more

Not at all. The Dynamic Workout Engine specializes in bodyweight exercises, but you can update your profile if you gain access to dumbbells or resistance bands.

How does the AI Fitness Coach work?

expand_more

Our integrated AI chat allows you to ask real-time questions about your routine, muscle recovery, and diet. It provides instant, personalized advice based on your fitness goals.

Is the Basic Fit plan really free forever?

expand_more

Yes! The Basic Fit plan gives you permanent access to our core workout dashboard and standard exercise library at no cost.

Can I use IntelliFit on my phone?

expand_more

Absolutely. The platform is fully responsive and designed to work seamlessly across desktop, tablet, and mobile browsers.
